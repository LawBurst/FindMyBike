## Research protocol

- Search current-generation manufacturer pages first, then full riding reviews (Blister, Pinkbike, BikeRadar, The Loam Wolf, Singletrack World, ENDURO, Mountain Bike Action, Bikepacking.com and comparable hands-on tests).
- Record source URL, publication/model generation, frame size, build, tires/pedals, manufacturer's claimed versus reviewer-measured status, and inclusion of rear shock, hardware and axle.
- Prefer reviewer-measured complete weights for a *specified build*, but these do **not** directly determine base model quality; normalized same-size/same-build comparisons require additional provenance.
- Frame-weight score eligibility requires known shock and hardware inclusion. If unknown, display only.
- Ensure none of the source measurements comes from old-generation models or distinct variants (example: Rascal SL data must not overwrite Rascal V3).
